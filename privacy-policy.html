Crumb

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 June 2026

Short version: Crumb has two modes. Without signing in, all your data stays on your device — we don't see it. If you sign in to use cross-device sync, your email and your app data are stored on a secure server (Supabase, in the EU) so you can access them from any device. We never use analytics, tracking, or advertising. You can delete your account and all data at any time.

1. Who we are

Crumb (the "App") is a sourdough companion application that helps you track your starter, manage recipes, and time your bakes. The App is operated by Malin Malm ("we", "us", "our"), based in Sweden.

Contact: FILLAN2013@HOTMAIL.COM

2. What data we handle

Crumb has two usage modes, with different data implications:

Anonymous mode (no sign-in)

If you use Crumb without creating an account, all your data is stored only on your device using your browser's built-in storage. Nothing is transmitted to our server. We have no record of you using the App at all.

Signed-in mode (cross-device sync)

If you create an account to sync data between devices, the following information is stored on our server (operated through Supabase — see Section 4):

  • Your email address (used for sign-in and password recovery)
  • Your password (never stored as plain text — only a cryptographic hash that even we cannot reverse)
  • Your app data, stored as a single JSON document: starters, feedings, recipes, reviews, bake history, app preferences. Substantially the same content you would otherwise have stored only on your device.
  • Technical metadata: the timestamp of your last sync, an auto-generated user ID

We use this data solely to provide the sync feature. We do not analyze it, sell it, share it for marketing, or use it for any other purpose.

Data we never collect, in either mode

  • We do not collect your name, address, phone number, payment details, or government IDs.
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or behavioral tracking.
  • We do not include third-party tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, or social-network pixels.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, calendar, or any other device sensors.

3. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

For anonymous mode users, we do not process personal data, so no legal basis is required.

For signed-in users, the legal basis under Article 6 of the GDPR is performance of a contract — you create an account to receive the sync service, and we process the minimum data needed to deliver that service to you.

4. Third-party services (sub-processors)

To provide the App, the following services may receive data:

  • Supabase (only when you are signed in) — Supabase hosts our authentication system and database. We have configured our project in an EU region, so your data is stored on servers within the European Union. Supabase processes data on our behalf under their privacy policy and offers GDPR-compliant data processing terms.
  • Google Fonts — when you load the App in a browser, two typefaces (Fraunces and Inter) are fetched from fonts.googleapis.com. Google may receive standard HTTP request data such as your IP address and browser user-agent. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • unpkg CDN — the App loads the React and Supabase JavaScript libraries from unpkg.com. The CDN provider may receive standard HTTP request data.
  • GitHub Pages (if you access the web version) — the App is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log standard web access data per GitHub's Privacy Statement.

If you install Crumb as a packaged native app from the App Store or Google Play, the asset-fetching requests to Google Fonts, unpkg, and GitHub Pages do not occur because those assets are bundled into the app package.

5. Notifications

If you enable browser notifications within Crumb, the App schedules local reminders for feeding times, dough fold intervals, peak windows, and bake-off times. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and are not sent through any external server. You can revoke notification permission at any time in your device settings.

6. Data retention

For anonymous users, data is retained on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App. We have no access to it and cannot delete it for you.

For signed-in users, your account and synced data are retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (see Section 7), all associated data is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

If a signed-in account is inactive for more than two years, we may delete the account and its data after sending a warning email to the address on file.

7. Your rights under GDPR (and equivalent laws)

If you are signed in, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access: All your data is visible within the App when signed in. To receive a copy as a JSON export, email us.
  • Right to rectification: Edit any starter, recipe, or review directly in the App. Changes sync to the server within seconds.
  • Right to deletion ("right to be forgotten"): Email us at the address below to request account deletion. We will permanently remove your account, email, and all synced data within 30 days. You can also reduce your data immediately by signing out and clearing browser storage.
  • Right to portability: Email us to request a JSON export of your data. A self-service export feature is planned.
  • Right to object / withdraw consent: Stop using the App; delete your account as above.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You may file a complaint with your national data protection authority. In Sweden, this is the Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

If you are an anonymous user, all your data is already on your device — you can view and delete it at any time without needing to involve us.

To exercise any right, email us at FILLAN2013@HOTMAIL.COM. We will respond within 30 days as required by GDPR.

8. Security

For signed-in users:

  • Passwords are stored only as cryptographic hashes (bcrypt), never in plain text.
  • All data transmission between your device and Supabase occurs over encrypted HTTPS / TLS.
  • Row-Level Security policies on our database ensure that a logged-in user can only read and write their own data, not anyone else's, even if they attempted to bypass the App.
  • Supabase operates SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure with industry-standard at-rest encryption.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever discover a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority without undue delay, as required by GDPR Article 33.

9. Children

Crumb is suitable for general audiences but is not directed at children. We do not knowingly create accounts for users under the age of 16 (the GDPR threshold for children's consent in Sweden). If you become aware that a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account.

10. International data transfers

Our Supabase project is configured to store data in the European Union. We do not knowingly transfer your personal data outside the EEA. Some of our other sub-processors (Google, GitHub) are based in the United States, but these do not receive your account data — only standard HTTP request metadata when you load the App's fonts or hosted page.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled — for example, by adding new features — we will update this policy and notify signed-in users through the App or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after a material change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Jurisdiction

This policy is governed by the laws of Sweden and the European Union. For any disputes, the competent courts in Sweden will have jurisdiction, except where mandatory consumer protection law provides otherwise.
